# The Bet · FinWise

> Module 1 · Ignite a PLG Motion. The growth hypothesis and where you're betting, plus the growth-loop visualization.

## Growth hypothesis

_The core belief: if we do X, then Y, because Z._
If FinWise prompts users to collaborate with a bookkeeper, accountant, or teammate after they experience their first valuable financial insight, then retention and trial-to-paid conversion will improve because FinWise becomes part of an ongoing financial workflow instead of a tool used once by a solo user.

_____

## The bet

_Where you focus, and what you're deliberately NOT doing._

I would bet on a collaboration loop.

FinWise already spends heavily on acquisition, while trial-to-paid conversion is only ~2% and one-year retention is 40%. My focus would therefore be on helping activated users build a repeatable workflow with another person.

I would deliberately not focus first on increasing paid acquisition or adding more onboarding steps.

The first experiment would be:

After FinWise surfaces the user's first personalized financial insight, prompt them to share it with or invite their bookkeeper, accountant, or teammate.
