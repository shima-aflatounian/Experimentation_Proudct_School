# The Solution · FinWise

> Module 2 · Acquisition & Activation. The onboarding solution and the Aha moment that makes value land.

## Aha moment

The user connects their real financial data and FinWise surfaces a meaningful insight they did not already know, for example:

“Your expenses increased 18%, while revenue increased only 4%. At this trend, monthly profit could be ~$2,900 lower next month.”

This is the moment FinWise becomes: “My money, finally clear.”

## Why this activates

The user moves from setting up FinWise to seeing immediate value from their own data.

Instead of showing a generic dashboard, FinWise identifies one important financial change and explains its impact.

This gives the user a reason to think:
“FinWise found something I would have missed. I want it to keep watching my business.”

That increases the likelihood that the user continues exploring the product, returns for new insights, and eventually converts from trial to paid.
The final collaboration step extends this Aha into a habit by encouraging the user to share and revisit financial insights with someone they trust.
